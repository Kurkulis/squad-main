# Economy

The economy governs how money flows in and out of your organization – how you earn, spend, and compete for resources.

---

## Income sources

| Source | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Match winnings | Prize money from league matches and tournaments |
| Sponsor deals | Passive income + bonuses for meeting sponsor objectives |
| Side missions | One-time payouts from special operations |
| Fanbase revenue | Scales with reputation and league standing |

---

## Expenses

| Expense | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Operator salaries | Paid per season or per match |
| Gear purchases | Weapons, attachments, armor, utility |
| Base upgrades | One-time construction costs |
| Staff salaries | Coaches, medics, scouts, analysts |
| Recruitment | Scout fees, auction bids, market purchases |

## Auction & Bidding System

Used for both **operators** (see [[Recruitment system]]) and **items** (gear, attachments, specialist tools).

The core mechanic is the same across all auction types.

### How it works

- Items and operators appear in the auction at set intervals
- Each listing has a **demand level** that determines how aggressively AI competitors will bid
- You set your bid without seeing what others will bid

### Demand levels

| Demand | AI behavior | Recommended strategy |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| Low | Passive – minimal bidding | Bid at or slightly above minimum |
| Medium | Moderate – some competition | Bid 20–40% above minimum |
| High | Aggressive – likely overbid | Commit a strong bid or skip |
| Hot | Maximum aggression | Expect a bidding war – only bid if critical |

### Risk curve

The more you bid above the base price, the lower the risk of being outbid – but the higher the budget impact. The player must weigh:

- **How much do I need this?**
- **Can I afford to lose the bid and wait for the next cycle?**
- **Is my current budget healthy enough to overpay?**

### Item auction tiers

| Tier | Examples | Typical demand |
|------|----------|----------------|
| Standard | Basic weapons, common attachments | Low |
| Advanced | High-end rifles, tactical armor | Medium |
| Specialist | Unique gadgets, rare gear | High |
| Premium | Sponsor-exclusive or event items | Hot |

---

## Budget management

- No hard debt system – you simply cannot bid or buy if funds are insufficient
- Running low on funds limits recruitment, gear upgrades, and base development simultaneously
- Sponsors can provide emergency contracts or advance payments as part of their deal terms
