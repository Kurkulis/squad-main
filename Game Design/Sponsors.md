# Sponsors

Sponsors are organizations that back your team in exchange for performance, playstyle, or public image. They provide economic benefits and unlock exclusive content, but come with obligations and allegiance conflicts.

---

## Sponsor tiers

### Major sponsors (Top 5)

The five largest and most prestigious sponsors in the game. Signing with one of them gives the biggest rewards but comes with strict exclusivity rules.

- Cannot sign operators affiliated with a rival major sponsor
- Cannot accept a competing major sponsor deal simultaneously
- Objectives tend to be demanding (win rate, aggression, consistency)
- Breaking objectives reduces standing and may result in contract termination

### Minor / independent sponsors

Smaller brands with fewer resources but no exclusivity restrictions.

- Can be stacked alongside major sponsor deals (up to a limit)
- Operators affiliated with minor sponsors can be signed freely
- Lower income, but flexible – useful early game or as supplements

---

## What sponsors provide

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Income | Flat payment per season or per win |
| Gear discounts | Reduced cost on specific item categories |
| Fanbase boost | Accelerated reputation growth |
| Training bonuses | Faster operator stat development |
| Special contracts | Access to exclusive gear or operators |
| Seasonal objectives | Bonus rewards for hitting specific milestones |

---

## Sponsor playstyle demands (tradeoffs)

Each sponsor has a preferred playstyle. Meeting their expectations increases standing and unlocks better rewards. Ignoring them risks losing the deal.

| Sponsor archetype | Wants | Penalizes |
|-------------------|-------|-----------|
| Aggressive brand | High kill counts, fast rounds | Passive or defensive play |
| Tactical brand | Clean wins, low casualties | Sloppy or chaotic play |
| Entertainment brand | Clutch moments, high drama | Boring dominant wins |
| Results brand | Win rate above all | Any loss streak |
| Development brand | Operator growth, young talent | Stagnation |

---

## Sponsor market & operator affiliation

Some operators on the [[Recruitment system#3. Sponsor Market|Sponsor Market]] are already contracted with sponsors.

- Signing a sponsor-affiliated operator means taking on their affiliation
- If the operator's sponsor is a rival major sponsor to your current one – the signing is blocked
- If the operator's sponsor is a minor or independent brand – signing is always allowed

### Conflict matrix

| Your sponsor | Operator's sponsor | Can sign? |
|--------------|-------------------|----------|
| Major | Same major | ✅ Yes |
| Major | Rival major | ❌ No |
| Major | Minor / independent | ✅ Yes |
| Minor | Any | ✅ Yes |
| None | Any | ✅ Yes |
