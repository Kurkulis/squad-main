There are three ways to recruit new operators. Each method has its own cost, time investment, risk, and reward profile.

---

## 1. Scout Assignment

Send your base Scout to search for a specific type of operator.

- You define the requirements: role, stats profile, preferred traits
- The Scout searches for a set number of in-game days and returns with a candidate offer
- **Cost:** hiring fee (paid upfront) + Scout salary
- **Time:** slow – requires waiting for the Scout to return
- **Result:** targeted and predictable – you get close to what you asked for
- Scout skill level affects the quality and accuracy of the candidate found

> Best used when you know exactly what role gap you need to fill.

---

## 2. Operator Auction

A shared public auction where randomly generated operators are listed at set intervals.

- Operators are fully random: random roles, stats, traits, and quality tier
- Multiple teams (including AI) bid simultaneously
- Each operator has a **demand level** – high-demand operators attract aggressive AI bidding

### Bidding mechanic

The key tension: the higher the demand, the more likely another team outbids you.

| Your bid | Risk of being outbid |
|----------|----------------------|
| Minimum  | Very high            |
| Moderate | Medium               |
| Aggressive | Low                |
| Maximum  | Very low             |

- You must decide how much to commit without knowing exactly what others will bid
- Overpaying wins the operator but strains your budget
- Underpaying is cheaper but unreliable

> The same demand-based bidding system applies to items and gear in the auction. See [[Economy]] for the full mechanic.

---

## 3. Sponsor Market

A large curated market of operators who have already signed sponsorship contracts.

- Operators here are higher quality on average, but come with sponsor affiliation
- Each operator is tied to one of the game's sponsors (major or minor)

### Signing restrictions

| Sponsor type | Rule |
|--------------|------|
| **Major sponsor** (top 5) | Cannot sign an operator affiliated with a rival major sponsor if you are already signed to one of the top 5 |
| **Minor / independent sponsor** | No restriction – can sign freely regardless of your current sponsor |

- This creates meaningful roster-building decisions tied to your sponsor allegiance
- Poaching a rival's sponsored operator is only possible if one or both parties are with minor sponsors

> Sponsor relationships, tiers, and conflict rules are documented in [[Game Design/Sponsors]].
